# Rote Playoffs

This folder holds entries for the Rote Playoffs hackathon.
The deadline is Sunday 6 September 2026, 8pm London time.

## House rules

1. Everything written here must be plain ASCII. No smart quotes, no em
   dashes, no arrows, no emoji, no accented characters. This covers code
   comments, markdown files, commit messages, and prompts.
2. Write like a person explaining something to a colleague. Short
   sentences. No marketing words, no filler, no hype.
3. Ask before any decision that is not obvious. Do not pick a name, a
   stack, or an approach on your own.
