WHAT TO TYPE
  frontmatter_completeness
      If the detail names tags or discoverability, the fix is a TOP-LEVEL
      `tags:` list. Tags under `metadata.discoverability.tags` do not count
      toward this signal, and that is the shape `rote workspace export`
      generates, so a play can carry nine tags and still be marked down. Keep
      the discoverability block as well; add the top-level one.
  output_format
      Declare an output contract: an `output:` block with format and
      destination, either at the top level or nested under `metadata.contract:`.
      Both satisfy this signal.

WHERE THESE NUMBERS COME FROM
  Every score here is rote's own, from `rote play score --format json`,
  which reports each signal with its weight and status and is authoritative.
  This play runs it across every play you have rather than one at a time,
  ranks the signals by what they are actually costing you, and adds the
  exact edit for the ones whose required shape is not obvious from the
  wording. Run `rote play score <main.ts>` yourself on any single play; the
  numbers will match, because they are the same numbers. Thanks to Chi blu
  in the Rote Playoffs Discord for pointing out that this command existed:
  an earlier version of this play reconstructed the rubric from the outside
  and got its structure wrong.
