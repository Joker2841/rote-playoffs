Two caveats I would rather state than have someone find.

The weights are not freely additive. With `metadata.version` absent, `discoverability` stops being scored, so tags plus version costs 0.25 rather than 0.37. I had that wrong at first and only caught it testing three removals at once.

Confidence: fitted on one control, then tried against five published Plays. Two predicted correctly first time; three were mispredicted and each revealed a signal I had missed. So two genuinely held-out data points, not five. One residual I cannot explain: `modiqo/hello` scores 0.45 where the model says 0.40, and no adjustment fixes it without breaking another Play.

Usage for the script:
```
python3 derive_rubric.py ~/.rote/flows/<a-play-of-yours-scoring-1.00> --pairs
```
