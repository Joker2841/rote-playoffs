rote 0.78.0, WSL2 Ubuntu 24.04.

`rote play validate` reports a capped score as a clean pass. Zero errors, zero warnings, `OK: Play validation passed!`, and a score of 0.65 with no indication of which signal is unsatisfied or what the missing field is worth.

Mine sat at 0.88 for hours looking like this:

```
  Quality score: 0.88 (Pass)
OK: Play validation passed!
  Signals evaluated: 7
```

Nothing there, or in `rote play lint`, says the cap came from `tags:` sitting under `metadata.discoverability.tags` instead of at the top level. That is the shape `rote workspace export` generates, so the exporter emits Plays the scorer marks down and neither tool mentions it.

Of the ten Plays installed here, six are capped. All six validate clean.

Weights, derived by taking a Play scoring 1.00 and removing one field at a time:

```
output_format             0.25  top-level output: OR metadata.contract.output
frontmatter_completeness  0.25  metadata.version
parametrization           0.13  at least one parameter
discoverability           0.12  top-level tags:
provenance_url            0.10  top-level source:
dag_structure             0.08  a steps: block
```

Worth nothing: `license`, `provenance.url`, `presentation_fixtures`, `depends_on`, `timeout_ms`, description length.

Two are surprising: `discoverability` ignores `metadata.discoverability.tags` and reads only top-level `tags:`, and `provenance_url` reads top-level `source:`, not `provenance.url`.
